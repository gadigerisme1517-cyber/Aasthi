AASTHI Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 July 2026

AASTHI ("we", "us", "our") operates the AASTHI mobile app, a property marketplace for buyers, renters and sellers in Kurnool, India. This page explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, and city, provided by you when you create an account (by email/password, Google Sign-In, or Facebook Login).
  • Listing photos: photos you upload when posting a property for sale or rent.
  • Verification documents: if you apply to become a verified seller, we collect a government ID and ownership proof document. These are private — only app administrators can review them, and they are never shown publicly.
  • Usage information: properties you save, enquiries or visit requests you send, and bug reports you submit.

How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account, and let sellers and buyers contact each other.
  • To show you relevant listings and let you save properties you're interested in.
  • To verify sellers who apply for a verified badge.
  • To respond to support requests and bug reports.

We do not use advertising trackers or analytics SDKs, and we do not sell your information to third parties.

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored using Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Cloud Storage), which runs on Google Cloud infrastructure. Access is restricted by security rules so that only you can read or write your own account data, and only app administrators can access verification documents.

Sign-in with Google or Facebook

If you sign in with Google or Facebook, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo from that provider to create your AASTHI account. We do not post to your Google or Facebook account or access anything beyond basic profile information.

Your choices

  • You can edit your profile information at any time from the Account screen.
  • You can delete your account from the app (Profile → Account → Delete account), which removes your profile data.
  • You can contact us (below) to request access to or deletion of your data.

Children's privacy

AASTHI is not directed at children under 18 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.
